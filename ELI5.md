# VIVISECT, explained simply

VIVISECT is an audio effect that turns incoming sound into controlled glitch sounds. It keeps a short, constantly updating piece of the sound in memory, then rearranges or damages little pieces of it.

Its different “surgeons” can repeat, reverse, freeze, reorder, or roughen slices. The Chaos and rhythm controls help decide whether those changes follow a steady beat or become more unpredictable.

It works like a live effects pedal inside a music program: it changes the sound passing through it, while leaving the original recording file alone. The 0.10.0 beta adds a resonant filter and a tempo-synced echo to polish the result, a key setting that keeps the pitch scatter in tune, a Dose knob that makes everything harder in one move, and a Capture button that records the result so you can drag it into your music program. Undo and Redo now cover your sound changes too.

The project README describes VST3, CLAP, and standalone builds (and LV2 on Linux).
